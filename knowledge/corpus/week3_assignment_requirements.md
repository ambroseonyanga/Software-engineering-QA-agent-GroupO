# BSE4104 Week 3 Context Engineering and RAG Requirements

Source: BSE4104 AI Agentic Capstone Assignment brief supplied to Group O.

Week 3 focuses on making the application answer from a controlled, traceable knowledge source instead of relying only on model memory.

## Required activities

- Assemble a controlled corpus of about 10 to 50 documents or equivalent records and record provenance.
- Implement ingestion, chunking or segmentation, indexing and retrieval.
- Construct model context from retrieved evidence and show sources in the response or trace.
- Create at least 15 RAG test questions covering answerable, partially answerable and deliberately unanswerable cases.
- Document at least three retrieval or grounding failures and their causes.

## Required Week 3 deliverables

- Corpus and Source Register.
- RAG architecture diagram.
- Working RAG pipeline with source grounding.
- 15-case RAG evaluation results.
- Week 3 progress report of one to two pages.

## Safety expectation

The system should use controlled project information and must handle unsupported questions without inventing information. High-impact actions remain outside the Week 3 retrieval workflow and require explicit human approval when introduced later.
